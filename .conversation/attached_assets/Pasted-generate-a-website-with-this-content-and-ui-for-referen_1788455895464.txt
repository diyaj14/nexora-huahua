## Hero

### Comprehensive Acoustic Engineering Services

From acoustic design and modelling to noise control and on-site testing, Nexora provides engineering solutions throughout the project lifecycle.

---

## 01 — Architectural Acoustics

### Designing Spaces That Sound Right

Architectural acoustics focuses on how sound behaves within and between spaces.

We develop acoustic strategies that improve sound quality, speech intelligibility, privacy, and occupant comfort while working within the architectural requirements of each project.

### Services Include

* Room acoustic design
* Reverberation control
* Sound insulation design
* Airborne sound insulation
* Impact sound insulation
* Façade acoustic assessment

---

## 02 — Mechanical Acoustics

### Controlling Noise From Building Services

Mechanical systems are essential to modern buildings, but HVAC equipment and mechanical plant can introduce unwanted noise and vibration.

Nexora assesses mechanical systems and develops practical acoustic solutions to control noise at its source and along its transmission path.

### Services Include

* HVAC acoustic assessment
* AHU, FAHU and FCU noise assessment
* Chiller noise assessment
* Chilled water pump noise assessment
* Fan and exhaust system noise assessment
* Cooling tower noise assessment
* Generator room acoustic assessment
* Mechanical plant room acoustic design
* Duct-borne noise assessment
* Breakout noise assessment
* Outdoor noise assessment

---

## 03 — Noise & Vibration Control

### Identify. Assess. Predict. Control.

Unwanted noise can affect occupants, neighbouring properties, building performance, and the surrounding environment.

Our engineering approach focuses on understanding the source and transmission of noise before developing appropriate mitigation strategies.

### Services Include

* Environmental noise assessment
* Noise impact studies
* Noise prediction
* Noise mitigation
* Sound transmission analysis

---

## 04 — Acoustic Testing

### Measure Performance. Verify Results.

Acoustic testing provides objective on-site measurements that help assess the acoustic performance of buildings and spaces.

Our testing services can be used to evaluate existing conditions, verify design performance, and support project requirements.

### Services Include

* Environmental noise surveys
* Sound insulation testing
* Reverberation time measurements
* Background noise measurement
* Room acoustic assessment

Where required, acoustic testing and measurements are used to assess performance.
